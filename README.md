# FP&A Intelligence & Revenue Analytics Dashboard

An end-to-end financial analytics project focused on quarterly revenue analysis, year-over-year (YoY) growth monitoring, company performance evaluation, and data quality assessment using Python, SQL, and Power BI.

## 📌 Project Overview

Financial planning and analysis (FP&A) teams need reliable financial data to monitor performance, identify unusual revenue movements, and support data-driven business decisions.

This project transforms financial reporting data into an interactive analytics solution featuring financial KPIs, quarterly revenue comparisons, growth analysis, and an analyst review worklist.

The dashboard is designed to help users explore company-level financial information and identify records that may require further investigation.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

* Analyze quarterly revenue and previous-year revenue.
* Examine year-over-year revenue growth.
* Compare financial performance across companies and reporting periods.
* Identify unusual growth patterns and sharp revenue declines for further review.
* Monitor data quality and missing comparison records.
* Build interactive dashboards for financial reporting and analysis.

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. FP&A Intelligence System

Provides an overview of financial reporting data through key performance indicators, quarterly revenue trends, analyst review categories, and data quality monitoring.

### 2. Revenue & Growth Analysis

Focuses on current versus previous-year revenue, YoY growth analysis, and company-level revenue comparisons.

### 3. Analyst Review & Risk Monitoring

Provides an analyst review worklist with company-level financial records, revenue comparisons, growth indicators, and data quality status. Interactive filters help users investigate specific records.

## ⚠️ Data & Interpretation Notes

* Revenue growth flags indicate records that may warrant investigation; they do not automatically indicate financial distress or incorrect data.
* Extreme growth percentages should be interpreted in the context of previous-year revenue and the underlying reporting data.
* Revenue measures must be interpreted at the appropriate company and reporting-period level.
* Dashboard results depend on the selected filters and the available dataset.
